      What SparkBench does and does not claim
      
  
        
    
      • One supported bench: a classic ESP32, a red LED on GPIO25, a green LED on GPIO26 and one small OLED.
        • 
    
      • Plans are at most 8 steps of 3 seconds with short uppercase labels. Meta proposes data; trusted local code renders the firmware.
        • 
    
      • A camera verdict covers sampled settled windows in the recorded view, not continuous behaviour or electrical measurements.
        • 
    
      • Unknown readings stay unknown. They never count as a pass or a fail.
        • 
    
      • Meta's advice and photo descriptions are guidance, not a pass. A photo cannot establish continuity, voltage or polarity.
        • 
    
      • The servo on the rig is never driven by this runtime and its angles are unmeasured.
        • 
    
      • Browsing saved work makes no model call and touches no hardware. Every Meta request and every bench action is an explicit button.
        • 
    
      • Evidence lives locally under runs/ with integrity hashes. Changed or missing evidence is shown as unavailable.
