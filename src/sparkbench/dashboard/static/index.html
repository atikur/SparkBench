                    What SparkBench does and does not claim
                    
  
                      
    
                    • One supported bench: a classic ESP32, a red LED on GPIO25, a green LED on GPIO26 and one small OLED.
                      • 
    
                    • Plans are at most 8 steps of 3 seconds with short uppercase labels. Muse Spark proposes data; trusted local code renders the firmware.
                      • 
    
                    • A camera verdict covers sampled settled windows in the recorded view, not continuous behaviour or electrical measurements.
                      • 
    
                    • Unknown readings stay unknown. They never count as a pass or a fail.
                      • 
    
                    • Parts and wiring ticks are your own report. They save automatically and do not check the hardware.
                      • 
    
                    • Muse Spark's advice and photo descriptions are guidance, not a pass. A photo cannot establish continuity, voltage or polarity.
                      • 
    
                    • The servo on the rig is never driven by this runtime and its angles are unmeasured.
                      • 
    
                    • The phone must stay still in its calibrated position. Frames from anywhere else are rejected, never read or guessed.
                      • 
    
                    • The agent never repairs anything. Muse Spark explains, you fix the bench, and the same run re-tests. The plan is never edited to match a fault.
                      • 
    
                    • Browsing saved work makes no model call and touches no hardware. A run starts only when you press the button, and every request and job it makes is saved.
                      • 
    
                    • Evidence lives locally under runs/ with integrity hashes. Changed or missing evidence is shown as unavailable.
